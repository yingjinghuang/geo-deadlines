## Distinguishing status from date

- A submission date in the past, a removed page, or an HTTP error is not alone confirmation of a closed or cancelled position; do not infer an extension either.
- An already-reviewed record with a real official close notice is checked in the main audit and excluded from this unresolved list.
- The separate [weekly freshness monitor](../.github/workflows/audit-freshness.yml) reports stale verification timestamps without editing them.
