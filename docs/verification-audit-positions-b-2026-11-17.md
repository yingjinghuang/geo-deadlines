# Recruitment verification audit B — 17 November 2026

This is the PolyU-through-Virginia-Tech portion of the full-catalog refresh. The inventory is fixed to the 31 recruitment records present at the start of this refresh; new discoveries are counted separately. Verification date: **17 November 2026, Europe/Vienna**.

**31/31 original records received an individual audit attempt.** Of these, **23 retained records were reverified** (12 unchanged and 11 corrected), **5 records were closed or archived and removed from the live catalog**, and **3 remain unresolved**. Nine separately discovered positions were verified and added. This is not a claim that all 31 originals are verified.

## Method and status rules

The audit reopened matching original university, HR, lab or PI sources, including entries whose dates had already elapsed. Public Workday job-detail responses supplied the original descriptions for Ottawa and McGill. Direct public HTTP retrieval supplied Illinois lab content, Stanford structured metadata and the UZH doctoral advert when the web extractor failed. A successful HTTP response alone did not qualify as verification: the matching role and relevant wording were inspected.

Final deadlines, review dates, application-portal end dates and discretionary late consideration are kept distinct. Date-only sources do not receive invented cutoff times. Rolling flags require current original wording supporting continued acceptance. Original-source access failures retain their old verification date. A past review date never establishes closure.

The five removals have explicit employer unavailability or an elapsed final deadline together with a gone original advert and a current employer-index/replacement check. The report records the reasoning and source URLs; prior YAML remains recoverable in Git history.
