## Presentation and semantics

The site's previous assumption that every completed application/review milestone means “Closed” was wrong for employers who **explicitly advertise rolling acceptance until filled**. This change adds `rolling: true` only to an active position `application` with `datetime: TBD`, validated in `scripts/validate-data.ts`. Such positions remain in Positions and show **Rolling / Open until filled** once any dated priority-review milestone passes. The original dated milestone remains in the timeline.

For discretionary or not-confirmed late applications (Cal Poly Pomona UAS, Cal State Fullerton, San José State, Stanford), a **separate TBD later-application milestone** retains visibility with an explicit “confirm eligibility” caveat instead of claiming a hard extension or confirmed open-until-filled status. The ISTA vacancy still displays a past 9 October cutoff and APPLY NOW contact; no extension has been invented.

Also fixed the previous discrepancy between server and client countdowns: both now display the earliest actionable public submission instead of overriding it with a later `primary` milestone. `primary` is only a tie-breaker for equal timestamps.
