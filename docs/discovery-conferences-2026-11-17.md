## IUGG source disagreement

The current [submission-specific author instructions](https://iugg2027korea.org/fairContents.do?FAIRMENU_IDX=25347&hl=ENG) explicitly repeat **16 February 2027, 23:59 HST (UTC−10)** and give the mathematically consistent equivalent **17 February, 18:59 KST (UTC+9)**. The page also says that submissions outside the online system or after this deadline will not be considered.

Two original sources still disagree:

- The organizer's [generic Important Dates page](https://iugg2027korea.org/fairContents.do?FAIRMENU_IDX=23441&hl=ENG) gives **16 February**, but labels the whole table as Korea Standard Time.
- The [IAG/GGOS announcement](https://geodesy.science/2026/call-for-abstracts-ggos-sessions-at-egu27-and-iugg-2027/) gives **14 February, 23:59 HST**. It also gives an event range beginning 11 July, whereas the host website and [IUGG newsletter](https://iugg.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IUGGej2611.pdf) give **16–22 July**.

The record uses the current detailed submission rule and preserves **all conflicting URLs plus the reason for preferring it**. This is a documented source-precedence choice, **not a claim that the original sources now agree**. The live host HTML and both relevant subpages were retrieved directly over HTTPS and inspected when the web retrieval tool could not open them.

## Search coverage and evidence handling

The sweep combined fresh searches with 60–180 day recency windows, society event calendars, original academic organizers, specialist conference sites, and accepted-workshop programs. It covered:

- ISPRS/FIG/IAG surveying, photogrammetry, deformation monitoring and specialist remote sensing.
- IEEE GRSS and regional remote-sensing series, plus forest/terrain and broad geosciences calls.
- Urban informatics, spatial AI, multimodal spatial reasoning and relevant transport/agriculture workshops.
- OSGeo regional/global leads and open-source geospatial developer events.
- Current record IDs and the archived uncertainty list to avoid duplicate parent/track cards or unsubstantiated restorations.

All added deadlines came from inspected original sources. Search snippets supplied leads, not the final evidence. Direct HTTPS retrieval was used for original HTML when needed; the SilviLaser program was visually inspected. OpenReview URLs were checked read-only; no submissions, registrations or messages were made.

The machine-readable handoff is `discovery-conferences-results.json` in the task workspace, with one row per addition, source URLs, reference IDs, metadata changes, exclusions and search coverage. Evidence captures are retained there for the IUGG/ICUI and workshop checks.
