> **Historical audit snapshot:** This report describes the original **140-entry** dataset before the 16 October concluded-recruitment cleanup. The current live catalog has **125 entries: 121 source-supported and 4 carrying explicit evidence caveats** after **15** closed recruitment records were removed. Original entries are retained in Git history; see [active-catalog maintenance report](active-catalog-maintenance-2026-10-16.md) and [same-day source follow-up](evidence-followup-2026-10-16.md). All original audit conclusions below remain the results as recorded for the earlier 140-entry snapshot.


**Audit date:** 2026-10-16 (Europe/Vienna). **Coverage:** 19/19 entries examined individually in this final batch. **Outcome:** 3 records established by direct authoritative evidence and given fresh `last_verified`; 16 records fully investigated but **not** newly certified, because primary current-source evidence was insufficient, inconsistent, or inaccessible. This distinction is deliberate: "all reviewed" is not "all verified."

The checks below record precisely what was and was not proven. Old deadlines can be historically credible without demonstrating present-day submission availability. Search-engine-rendered snippets of an official publisher site remain **publisher-index evidence**, not equivalent to opening the publisher's up-to-date detail page. A directly opened original editor announcement, an employer's own official expired-status record, or a publisher issue with independently established full details has stronger evidentiary standing.
