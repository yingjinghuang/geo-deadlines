## Other candidates considered but not added

- **GIScience, ACM SIGSPATIAL, AGILE, ICC, IGARSS, ISPRS GSW, EGU, AAG, CVPR, Spatial Statistics, etc.** are already in the catalog, so no duplicate 2027 entries.
- Some **ICGDA / RSMIP / SGEM-style** meetings have advertised calls. These were not prioritized in this quality-focused pass over venues with stronger alignment to GIScience, urban computation, or major community series. This is a scope/curation decision, not a claim that their announcements are illegitimate.
- Future workshop or 2028 venue announcements without a confirmed, current CFP were not treated as actionable paper submission dates.

## Freshness policy

Dates are stored **as published**; timezones are supplied only when an official organizer explicitly names them. Provisional dates and future-only announcements are labeled. The usual **3-day re-verification cadence** applies to all eight newly added entries. This discovery does not replace a full ongoing audit of the previously tracked catalog.
