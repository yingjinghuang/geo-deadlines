# Additional journal discovery — 17 November 2026

## Result and scope

This follow-up discovery adds **30 distinct special issues across 10 journals** and makes **one documented precision correction** to an existing collection. The starting point was main `2b2df71`, with 144 total opportunities and **42 special issues**. This is a search for omissions after the full audit already completed in this session; it does not claim to repeat that audit.

Candidate identities were compared with all **42 current special issues** and **18 distinct historically removed special-issue IDs**, using titles, journal/editor identity and dedicated CFP URLs. Similar foundation-model topics in different journals were checked as separate named calls. Existing CEUS fire research and the other tracked collections were not added again.

The search used recent publisher-focused queries for GeoAI, GIScience, remote sensing, Earth observation, spatial data science, urban analytics and spatial health. Every accepted item is supported by an inspected original publisher page or CFP PDF. Direct requests recovered five MDPI details and two Nature collections that the search reader could not open; their actual HTML, issue identity, editors and deadlines were checked. Search snippets were leads only.

## Submission-stage distinctions

- **Two JSTARS conference collections require an expanded paper presented at the named 2026 conference.** Their eligibility is explicit in the description and deadline note. These are journal manuscript deadlines, not reopened conference deadlines.
- **Three JSTARS calls have future submission opening dates:** remote sensing image compression and hyperspectral interpretation open 1 December 2026; embodied agentic UAV systems open 1 January 2027. Their opening milestones use `type: other`, and the manuscript closing dates remain separate. As of this report they are announced opportunities, not already-open portals.
- **SAGE community health:** the publisher says submissions are open and invites GIS/GeoAI research without a stated GeoSus attendance condition. The institutional announcement and publisher agree on 8 June 2027.
- **Nature cutoff precision:** two inspected Scientific Reports About pages explicitly close the system at 00:00 GMT on the day after the advertised date. Exact next-day closing instants are recorded, with the advertised calendar dates preserved in notes. This is a precision correction, not a one-day extension. No times were inferred for the other calls.
