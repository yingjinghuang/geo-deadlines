Added **seven faculty opportunities** after the full-catalog audit at baseline `2b2df71` (144 opportunities, including 64 recruitment records). These are new discoveries, not additional claims of re-verifying the baseline. No existing recruitment record was edited or removed by this pass.

Third-party job sites, professional-society feeds and public faculty posts supplied leads; they did not settle a disputed deadline or current availability. Dates were inspected on 17 November 2026. The discovery sweep is broad but cannot certify that every global vacancy is indexed or publicly discoverable.

## Original-source recovery and precision decisions

The Liège PDF was retrieved successfully from the university using an ordinary public HTTP request even though the web rendering service returned an error. All four pages were extracted and read, including the application cutoff, appointment conditions and lecturer-grade statement. A university-authored syndicated copy was a discovery aid, not the final deadline source.

CUHK's full job description is embedded as percent-encoded text in the public Taleo HTML. The university HR page links to the `cu_career_teach` career section; that exact section returned the matching vacancy and its complete description. An incorrect `cu_career_academic` path returned a career-section error and was not interpreted as vacancy closure. Different dates on secondary copies did not override the employer's 11 December closing date.

UFTM's current-process page embeds its public publication system. Reading its linked JavaScript identified the normal read-only `getPublicacoesAtivas` request; the response for section 103 and search `33/2026` returned active publication **17716**, its application period, application link and all notice attachments. The current record links the same original edital. The first correction changes the subject area of **Lotação 04**, not this Geography vacancy. Publication-display start/end fields were not used as application dates. The PDF's document-signature time was not mistaken for an application cutoff.

No exact closing time was inferred from an institution's timezone, from an aggregator convention, or from a document timestamp. Toronto and Liège are the only additions with explicitly supported exact cutoffs. UMass and Harvard have separate soft review/recommended dates and continuing recruitment notes.
