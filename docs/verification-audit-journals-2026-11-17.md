# Journal and special-issue verification audit — 17 November 2026

## Coverage and interpretation

The starting catalog contained **36 special issues**. Every one received an individual source-review attempt: **33 have inspected original evidence** and **3 remain unresolved**. The 33 comprise **7 verified unchanged**, **9 verified corrected** (source links, editor metadata or submission-stage notes), and **17 closed or archived** historical calls. All 36 records are retained. No original manuscript deadline needed to be changed; a verified date means the cited evidence was read, not that every publisher submission portal was tested.

The three unresolved Taylor & Francis records retain `last_verified: "2026-11-04"`; their public notes explain the 17 November retrieval failure. The other 33 advance to `2026-11-17`. All submission deadlines remain date-only with `timezone: null`. Historical calls are archived by their elapsed dates; their YAML deadline `status: active` identifies the retained deadline version and does not mean submissions remain open.

The mandatory watchlist sweep covered **13/13 journals as review attempts**, with publisher access limitations explicitly recorded below. **Six new calls** were added after inspection of original publisher or institutional guest-editor evidence. A source returning HTTP 200 with a “Site Unavailable” body was treated as inaccessible. Search results were used to discover leads, never to refresh a verification date.

## Material corrections

- Five readable Taylor & Francis CFPs now use the publisher’s working `callforpapers.taylorandfrancis.com` URLs after checking the redirects. The three inaccessible CFPs retain their previous dates and a visible recheck warning.
- IJGIS **Critical Challenges in GeoAI** and **Ethics in Geospatial AI and Data Science** now explain that the future full-paper date follows an elapsed abstract stage and an invitation; it is not a new unrestricted entry point. The existing Annals of GIS stage distinction was confirmed.
- Transactions in GIS spatial-data sharing now cites its dedicated CFP instead of repeating the index citation. Missing editor names were added to that entry, GIScience education, spatiotemporal planning, and both historical CEUS records.
- For IJGI urban digital twins, the current dedicated page and live index both show **31 March 2027** and the index says open for submission. The earlier **23 December 2026** discrepancy remains documented as historical: the old homepage banner was not found on the present homepage and the issue-specific flyer was inaccessible. No unexplained conflict was described as independently resolved.
- The new SWOT collection uses **28 February 2027**, the date in both fields of the actual current publisher CFP. The older 30 November 2026 search-index date was rejected.
