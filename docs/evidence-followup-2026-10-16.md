## Live inventory and decisions

**125 active YAML records remain**, after 15 concluded recruitment listings were removed earlier today. **121/125 now have sufficiently grounded deadline/source evidence for the fields being displayed; 4 need a caveat**. The original 140-record audit was an earlier snapshot and is not a current to-do checklist.

This pass updated **nine special-issue `last_verified` fields to 2026-10-16**, with the evidence and limitations written into their individual notes. **Two additional officially contradictory records were reviewed and dated 2026-10-16, but are NOT counted among the 121 unambiguous records**. The remaining two historical issue records have their prior `last_verified` timestamps, because direct source evidence for the precise cutoff is insufficient. No speculative deadline/timezone change was made.

The core verification rule is straightforward: a publisher's **own live CFP index**, when it explicitly matches the issue title, journal, editors, and submission date, is authoritative evidence for the *published date* even if the per-issue detail returns 403. An original, personally authored editor CFP and a responsible institutional announcement are also evidence. A **search-engine snippet copied from a publisher URL**, a mismatched call, or an arbitrary third-party posting alone is not enough. `last_verified` denotes when the cited evidence was reviewed, **not an assurance that the publisher can never announce a new extension**.
